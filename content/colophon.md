> “A colophon is a page or section (typically in a footer) of a site that describes how the site is made, with what tools, supporting what technologies, and often published on personal sites at a top level /colophon page.” — https://indieweb.org/colophon

This site—when created in March 2025—was a huge deal for me. My go-to site builder has always been [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/) since 2004. Heck, I even [wrote a couple of books about Squarespace](https://kris.black/work/books).

For those who are curious, below is everything I use to make this website and [all my other websites](https://www.krisblackstudio.com/).

## Web Resources

- [**Google Workspace**](https://referworkspace.app.goo.gl/fG7Y) for email, calendar, and online file storage
- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/) for managing and registering domain names
- [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/) for powering this website

## Software & Apps

- [Affinity Suite](https://affinity.serif.com/en-us/) is used to process any images and graphics. I even use Affinity software to make the writeDRAWcode_ logo and logomark. *FYI - I stopped using Adobe Creative Suite/Cloud in 2023.*
- Fonts on writeDRAWcode_ are set to use your device's [default system font](https://css-tricks.com/snippets/css/system-font-stack/), falling back to Helvetica Neue and other sans-serif fonts.
- [Nova](https://nova.app/) by Panic to write custom CSS/LESS code for styling the site beyond Squarespace’s built-in design features. I use another app, [CodeKit](https://codekitapp.com/), to compile my CSS for easy copy-n-paste into Squarespace Custom Code area.
- [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) for writing blog posts.
- I have a subscription to [SetApp](https://setapp.sjv.io/AWEz4N) which gives me access to a whole suite of apps on the Mac. I like evaluating new apps to see if I can streamline my productivity or enhance my workflows.
